LiveTool Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Revised: 2026

1. Fundamental Architecture: Zero-Server Processing

At LiveTool, your digital privacy is our core engineering foundation. All tools—including the AI Background Remover, AI 4K Image Enhancer, HD Image Resizer, Image Compressor, Unit Converters, and Financial Calculators—operate exclusively inside your local browser memory (Client-Side). Your photos, documents, and data are NEVER uploaded, stored, or processed on any remote server or database.

2. What Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No File Uploads: When you select or drop a photo into an image tool, the file is read locally via HTML5 File API and processed on your device GPU/CPU.
  • No Account Credentials: LiveTool does not require registration, passwords, phone numbers, or subscriptions.
  • No Sensitive Financial Logs: Numbers entered into EMI or Tax calculators exist only in local browser state and disappear when you refresh or close the tab.

3. Local Storage & Preferences

LiveTool may store non-sensitive UI preferences (such as your chosen language or theme) locally on your device via localStorage. You may clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

4. Direct Inquiries & Contact Details

If you email us at Lifetooloffice@gmail.com, your email address and message will only be used to respond to your support request. We will never sell, rent, or distribute your email address to third parties.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements. The latest revised date will always be visible at the top of this page.
