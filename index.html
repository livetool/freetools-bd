BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.

Are the tools really free? +
Yes! Every single tool is 100% free with unlimited access, zero subscriptions, and no hidden account sign-ups.
Are my images uploaded? +
When you resize, compress, crop or convert an image, it is handled by your browser's canvas. Nothing needs to travel to a server for that work.
Can I use the results on your phone? +
Absolutely. The entire LiveTool interface is fully responsive, lightweight, and engineered for modern iOS and Android mobile browsers.
What is coming next? +
We are currently building dedicated client-side PDF utilities, batch image converters, and specialized Unicode Bangla typography formatters.
